B2B SaaS · International

280% Organic Traffic Growth for B2B SaaS Platform in 8 Months

Industry: SaaS / Project Management Timeline: 8 months Market: Global (English)

The Challenge

A mid-sized B2B SaaS company had solid product-market fit but struggled with organic visibility. Their website suffered from slow load times and thin content.

  • Slow page load times (LCP over 4 seconds)
  • Thin content with minimal entity coverage
  • Poor internal linking structure

Strategy & Execution

  • Technical SEO: Fixed Core Web Vitals and crawl errors.
  • Entity Optimization: Mapped brand and product entities.
  • Content Strategy: Published 24 pieces of content across 4 pillar topics.

Results Achieved

+280% Organic traffic
47 Keywords top 3
+85% Demo requests
E-commerce · Nepal

320% Revenue Growth for E-commerce Store

Industry: Retail / Fashion Timeline: 9 months Market: Nepal
